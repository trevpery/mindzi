03

Children’s data

Many Mindzi products are meant for children, but purchases and registrations must be completed by a parent, guardian, teacher or authorised adult.

We do not ask children to independently submit personal information for e-book purchases. Where a future course, certificate, competition, workshop or child product requires a child’s name, age/class, learning progress or other details, such information will be collected only through an adult and only for the stated purpose.

We do not knowingly use children's personal data to target advertisements, create marketing profiles, or advertise directly to children. Our marketing activities are intended for parents, guardians, teachers and schools who make purchasing or enrolment decisions on behalf of children. We do not sell children's personal data to any third party.

04

How we use information

  • To create and confirm orders.
  • To process payments through Razorpay or another payment processor.
  • To generate GST invoices and maintain accounting records.
  • To deliver e-books, course links, downloads, resources, certificates or product access.
  • To provide customer support, corrections, refunds or replacements where applicable.
  • To send important transactional updates related to purchases or courses.
  • To improve our products, website, content and user experience.
  • To comply with applicable Indian laws, tax obligations and lawful requests.

We may send updates about related Mindzi educational resources, courses, books, events or initiatives. You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time.

05

Who we share information with

  • Payment processors: Razorpay or other payment providers process payment information. We do not store card details.
  • Email and delivery tools: Used to send invoices, receipts, e-book links, course access and support messages.
  • Cloud, storage and automation tools: Used for order records, invoice PDFs, download links and internal operations.
  • Accountants, auditors and legal advisors: Where needed for tax, accounting, compliance or dispute handling.
  • Delivery/logistics partners: If physical products are introduced, only the information needed for shipping will be shared.
  • Authorities: Where required by law, court order or government request.

We do not sell or rent personal data.

06

How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as it is required to provide our services or to comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting and regulatory obligations.

If you request deletion of your personal data, we will delete it wherever reasonably possible.

However, certain records such as invoices, payment records, tax documents, or information required for fraud prevention or dispute resolution may need to be retained for the period required by law.

Where immediate deletion is not possible, we will explain why and restrict the use of that information to those purposes only.

07

Your rights and choices

You may request access, correction, update, withdrawal of consent, or deletion of personal data, subject to legal and operational requirements. Parents or guardians may make requests relating to a child’s personal data.

To make a request, email hello@mindziai.com with the subject line Data Request. We may need to verify your identity before acting on the request.

08

Cookies and analytics

We use essential cookies and similar technologies to ensure that the website functions correctly, remembers basic preferences, improves website performance, and protects the website against spam, fraud, unauthorised access and misuse.

We may also use website analytics and advertising technologies (such as Meta Pixel or similar tools) to understand how visitors use our website, measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns, and improve our services.

These tools help us reach parents, guardians, teachers and schools who may be interested in Mindzi products.

We do not knowingly use children's personal data for targeted advertising or commercial profiling.

09

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. Payment details are processed by payment providers and are not stored by Mindzi. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep data protected and access limited.
